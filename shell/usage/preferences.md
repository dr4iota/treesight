# Preferences

Four settings, remembered on this device.

- **Theme**, in the header — **Light**, **Dark**, or **Auto**, which follows
  the system and is the default. The button shows the one chosen; click moves
  to the next.
- **Line numbers** — on or off, for highlighted source: a switch at the right
  end of the line above the source, beside its size and date.
- **Sidebar**, in the header — show or hide the left side. Hidden, the file
  fills the window. On a narrow window the same button slides the sidebar in
  over the page instead.
- **Zoom** — how large the file, folder or start page is drawn. `Ctrl+=` and
  `Ctrl+−`, or `Ctrl+wheel`, step the text a pixel at a time below 16px, two
  below 24px and four above, from 8px to 48px; `Ctrl+0` goes back to 100%. On
  a touch screen a two-finger pinch moves it smoothly, and a quick tap with two
  fingers goes back to 100%. The header and the sidebar keep their size.

A switch that is on is lit; its word does not change. Each button shows its
mark beside its word. When the header runs out of room the words give way, all
of them at once, and the marks stay; then the path moves to a line of its own.

## Printing

**Print** in the header of a rendered file or a source view, or `Ctrl+P`. The
printed page drops the header, the sidebar and the status line, and uses the light
theme.

## Hidden files

Dotfiles are not listed.
